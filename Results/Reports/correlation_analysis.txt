SPEARMAN CORRELATION ANALYSIS
======================================================================

Purpose
-------
This analysis examines exploratory associations between demographic/gameplay variables, overall performance, and cognitive-domain performance.

Variables
---------
- age
- weekly_gaming_hours
- sleep_hours_last_night
- games_played
- total_plays
- overall_performance
- overall_best_performance
- Memory
- Attention
- Reflex
- Perception
- Learning
- Reasoning

Important methodological notes
-------------------------------
- Spearman correlation is used because the sample is relatively small and relationships may not be normally distributed.
- Pairwise complete observations are used for each correlation.
- Correlations involving constant variables are reported as NaN because they are mathematically undefined.
- Correlation does not establish causation.
- Cognitive-domain assignments are based on the game design and should not be interpreted as clinically validated cognitive measurements.
